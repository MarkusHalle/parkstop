| D20 | 2026-11-12 | **O1 resolved.** Default recipient stays the enforcement body from the resolver (district body or FÖRI); the e-mail is a *bejelentés* asking them to check and act (SPEC 7 template A). The owner read and approved the final Hungarian wording, including "Szabálytalanság:" instead of "Szabálysértés:" and "Szükség esetén tanúként rendelkezésre állok." | Legal research (Opus) + Fable sceptic, all on net.jogtar.hu / njt.jog.gov.hu / police.hu: stopping and waiting breaches are petty offences (Szabs. tv. 224. § (1)); police and public-space inspectors may fine on the spot, also with the driver absent (39. § (1)–(2), 100. §), but only on their own observation (39. § (2), 99. § (1)); the keeper's strict liability does not reach stopping/waiting (Kkt. 21. § (1), 410/2007 annexes; unchanged in the Kkt. version from 2026-12-31). So no body can fine from a citizen's photo; the enforcement body can act while the car is there, and the police station of the place is the petty-offence addressee for an after-the-fact report (78. §; BRFK is not). Police e-mail acceptance is unconfirmed — a 2020–2024 police guide allowed e-mail, the current one (2025-10-30) points to form IN-100100, which needs a portal login | owner, on the research |
