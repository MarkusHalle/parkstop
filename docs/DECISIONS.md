| D7 | 2026-11-11 | E-mail hand-off: share sheet on phones; `mailto:` plus photo download/copy on PC; `.eml` draft as an extra for classic Outlook and Apple Mail | A `mailto:` link cannot carry attachments. `.eml` with `X-Unsent: 1` is unreliable in new Outlook and unsupported in Thunderbird, so it cannot be the main path | Claude, to be confirmed by spikes S1 and S2 |

| D9 | 2026-11-11 | Add an on-device number-plate reader (fast-alpr ONNX models via ONNX Runtime Web); constraint C1 reworded from "no AI" to "no cloud AI, no generative AI" with this single exception. Alternatives rejected: Tesseract.js (weaker on plates, user must frame the plate), the browser's TextDetector (Chrome behind a flag only), cloud plate APIs (key, limits, photo leaves the device) | owner |

| D16 | 2026-11-12 | Spike S3 result on the owner's 20 photos (run in Chrome on the owner's PC): **18 of 20 read exactly (90 %) — the accuracy gate (≥ 85 %) is met.** Median 0.32 s per photo on the PC. Of the 18 reads above the 0.90 fill-in threshold, 17 were right and 1 wrong.
